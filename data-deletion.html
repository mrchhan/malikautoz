Data Deletion Instructions

Malik Autoz — Motorcycle Parts Shop Management System

Last updated: September 2026

If you've messaged Malik Autoz on Instagram or Facebook, placed an order with us, or otherwise have information on file with us, you can ask us to delete it at any time. This page explains how.

How to request deletion

  1. Email malikautoz@outlook.com, or send us a message on Facebook or Instagram, with the subject or first line "Delete my data".
  2. Include enough for us to find your record — e.g. the phone number you ordered with, or simply "please delete the Instagram conversation from [date]" if your request is about a message.
  3. We'll confirm we've received your request within 2 business days.

What gets deleted

  • Instagram/Facebook messages: the message content and any identifiers tied to that conversation are permanently removed from our systems.
  • Customer records: your name, phone number, and address are removed from our shop records, unless we're legally required to keep a sales/tax record for a limited period — in that case we'll tell you what's retained and why.

Timeline

Acknowledged: within 2 business days

Completed: within 14 days of your request

Automatic deletion

Even without a request, Instagram/Facebook messages are never kept indefinitely: if our shop goes 90 days without receiving a new message, the entire message backlog is automatically cleared from our systems.
