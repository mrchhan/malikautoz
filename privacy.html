Privacy Policy

Malik Autoz — Motorcycle Parts Shop Management System

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information Malik Autoz collects, how it is stored, and how it is used — including when you message our shop on Instagram or Facebook. Malik Autoz is a single motorcycle parts shop based in Rawalpindi, Pakistan; this system is used to run that one shop, not to serve other businesses.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers two groups of people:

  • Customers who visit the shop, place orders, or message us on Instagram/Facebook.
  • Shop staff who use the internal management system to record sales, inventory, and deliveries.

2. Information we collect

From in-person or phone orders

Name, phone number, and address, when provided — used to record a sale, arrange delivery, or maintain a running account with a regular customer.

From Instagram and Facebook messages

When you message our Page or Instagram account, we receive the message text and an identifier Meta assigns to your account for that conversation (a Page-Scoped ID or Instagram-Scoped ID). We do not receive your real name, phone number, or any other Instagram/Facebook profile information unless you tell us directly in the conversation.

We use this to see what you're asking about and to follow up — typically to arrange an order and delivery. Message data is held only as long as needed to handle your order, and is not used for advertising or shared with any third party.

From invoices or documents you send us

If a supplier or customer sends an invoice image or document, our system may use Google's Gemini AI to read details like item names, quantities, and prices from it. Google's own privacy terms govern how Gemini processes that request; we do not send this content anywhere else.

3. How information is stored

Most shop data (sales records, inventory, customer and supplier lists) is stored locally on the shop's own device, in the browser's storage — it is not sent to any external server by default. If the shop owner turns on optional multi-device sync, that same data is also stored in a private cloud database accessible only with a device-specific access key.

Instagram/Facebook messages are held in a small, private message queue (hosted on our infrastructure) until shop staff review them and take action, after which they can be deleted (see Section 5).

4. How information is used

  • To record and fulfill a sale or delivery order
  • To respond to a question or order sent via Instagram/Facebook message
  • To maintain accurate inventory and accounting records
  • To contact a customer about an existing order (e.g. delivery status)

We do not sell customer information, use it for advertising, or share it with any third party except where legally required.

5. Data deletion

You can ask us to delete any information we hold about you — a message you sent us, or your customer record — at any time. See our Data Deletion Instructions page for how to request this.

6. Employee accounts

Staff log in with a PIN, which is stored as a salted, one-way cryptographic hash — never as plain text, and never recoverable by us or anyone else. We do not collect employee personal data beyond what's needed to run the shop (name, role, and optionally a phone number).

7. Children's privacy

This system is a business tool for running a motorcycle parts shop and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If how we handle data changes meaningfully, we'll update this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
